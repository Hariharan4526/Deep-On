## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Don't get stuck in tutorial hell** - Build projects while learning
2. **Understand concepts, don't memorize code** - You can always Google syntax
3. **Start simple, iterate often** - Your first model won't be perfect
4. **Debug systematically** - Print shapes, visualize data, check one layer at a time
5. **Join a community** - Learning with others is faster and more fun
6. **Read error messages carefully** - They're trying to help you
7. **Use Google and Stack Overflow** - Everyone does, including experts
8. **Take breaks** - Your brain needs time to consolidate learning
