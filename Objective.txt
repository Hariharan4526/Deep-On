Deepfake & AI Content Detection

Background
The rapid advancement of Artificial Intelligence has enabled the creation of highly realistic synthetic media such as deepfake videos, AI-generated images, cloned voices, and manipulated digital content. These technologies are increasingly misused for misinformation, impersonation, financial fraud, and cybercrime.

Objective
Develop a practical prototype system that detects AI-generated or manipulated media and helps investigators verify digital authenticity quickly.

Solution Scope
- Detection of deepfake videos, images, and audio
- Identification of potentially AI-generated content
- Metadata and content-level artifact analysis
- Confidence scoring for authenticity vs manipulation
- Near real-time analysis for field usage

Evaluation Parameters
- Detection accuracy and robustness
- Ability to estimate likely generation/manipulation source (heuristic)
- Scalability and performance
- Ease of use for non-technical investigators
- Practical applicability for digital investigations

Expected Outcome
A prototype tool that assists investigators in identifying manipulated or synthetic digital media with transparent evidence and confidence scores.

Project Conclusion and Build Direction
To implement this objective, the project will be built in phased form:
1) MVP prototype (current phase): single-file analysis for image/video/audio with confidence scoring and evidence reporting.
2) Model-enhanced phase: integrate trained deep learning classifiers for face-swap, diffusion-generated image, and synthetic voice detection.
3) Forensic phase: improve explainability (artifact maps), calibration, and reporting workflow for legal/investigation support.
4) Deployment phase: API/dashboard + near real-time pipeline for operational usage.

Success Criteria for MVP
- Accept media input and classify as likely authentic/manipulated
- Produce confidence score and traceable evidence features
- Export machine-readable JSON report for investigators
- Maintain modular design for future model upgrades
